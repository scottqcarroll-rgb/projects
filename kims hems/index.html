What we do

Our Services

Wedding Dresses

Bridal gown alterations — hemming, bustles, bodice fittings, straps, lace repairs, and full custom resizing so your dress fits like it was made for you.

Prom & Formal Gowns

Perfect fits for prom, homecoming, and special occasion gowns — hemming, taking in, straps, and bustle work so you look flawless on the big night.

Suits & Formal Wear

Precision tailoring for suits, tuxedos, and formal attire — jackets, trousers, sleeves, and waist adjustments for a sharp, confident fit.

Everyday Alterations

Hemming, taking in, letting out, and repairs for all your clothing — shirts, pants, dresses, and more.
